# Provenance

Provenance says who or what made a piece of work: generated, generated then edited, or made by a person.

**Variants:** inline (`l-prov`, in metadata lines) · overlay (`l-prov--overlay`, bottom-left on artwork) · edited (`l-prov--edited`, muted).

**Icon:** `aperture` (a camera iris) marks generated work across Lorven. It replaces the generic sparkle on purpose: it reads as "captured by the studio's machine", it is ownable, and it carries no implied magic.

**Rules:** provenance is information, not decoration — no colour, glow or gradient. It persists after acceptance ("Generated · edited by Meera") so reviewers know what they are approving. Never hide it on export previews.
