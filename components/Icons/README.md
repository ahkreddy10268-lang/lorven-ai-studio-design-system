# Icons

The Lorven icon set: 44 original icons on a 24px grid with a 1.5px round stroke, drawn for filmmaking work and the three status families.

**Use:** `<span data-icon="storyboard" data-size="20"></span>` after `components/bundle.js` loads, or `Lorven.icon(name, size, label)`. Icons inherit `currentColor`. Pass a label only when the icon stands alone and carries meaning; otherwise they are `aria-hidden`.

**Sizes:** 16 in chips, captions and dense rows; 20 in buttons, tabs and menus; 24 in navigation; 32 for empty states only.

**Rules:** outline by default; fill only as meaning inside status glyphs. Keep icons with their family (approval circles, currency links, attention targets). Product icons never stand in for official product marks. No sparkle for AI.
