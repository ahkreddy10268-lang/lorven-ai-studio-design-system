# Directions

Directions compares the two art directions on identical components and records why Direction B is the product direction.

Direction A (cinematic editorial) uses the master brand's gold as the action colour; next to the amber **Check** status the two become hard to tell apart, and in light mode gold must darken to pass contrast, at which point it is the Check colour. Direction B (Lorven Studio) keeps action in electric blue and leaves every status hue unambiguous. A's warmth moves to brand surfaces instead.
